## The across-lives mechanism

**R1. Sustenance between bodies (SN 44.9 Vacchagotta-fire, Thanissaro ATI).**
"Vaccha, when a being sets this body aside and is not yet reborn in another
body, I designate it as craving-sustained, for craving is its sustenance at
that time." Status: VERIFIED (box file `gochema/essays/rebirth-explained/
quotes/`; gochema peer-review v1.2). Use for: what carries over — fuel,
not passenger.

**R2. Conception needs three; consciousness does not transmigrate
(MN 38 Mahatanhasankhaya, Thanissaro ATI).** "The descent of the embryo
occurs with the union of three things... mother & father... her season...
a gandhabba is present." Same sutta corrects Sati: no "same consciousness"
that runs and wanders. Status: VERIFIED (box file). Use for: the early
mechanics + the no-traveler rule stated by the text itself.

**R3. Conduct, not deathbed flash, conditions rebirth (Abhidhammatthasangaha
§13 footnote, via gochema ESSAY v1.2).** "What actually conditions rebirth
is that which is experienced during the javana process" — not the last
decease-thought. Status: FLAGGED check:true — gochema's own audit lists no
banked source file yet ("bank the Narada/Bodhi passage"). Quote shape is
per their citation-verified script; confirm wording before video. Use for:
the karma-brings-the-trigger beat (conduct lays the groove).

## Refusing the wrong questions

**R4. Inappropriate attention (MN 2, via Thanissaro Truth of Rebirth file).**
"Am I? Am I not? What am I? What was I in the past? What will I be in the
future?" — declared the kind of attention that ignores the four noble
truths. Plus Yamaka (SN 22.85): Sāriputta dismantles annihilationism
aggregate by aggregate — can't pin down the Tathagata even now, so no
post-mortem declaration. Status: VERIFIED (box files). Use for: why the
essay refuses "where do I go" and "who travels" as posed.

**R5. The fair opponent (Ajita Kesakambali, DN 2).** "There is no fruit or
result of good or bad actions... With the breakup of the body, the wise and
the foolish alike are annihilated." Status: STANDARD (gochema FAQ bank).
Use for: one breath of steelman — the tradition kept its best opponent's
words.

**R6. Working hypotheses (Thanissaro, Truth of Rebirth, via gochema ESSAY
v1.2 citation-verified).** "Prior to awakening, you can't know these things
for sure... it's wisest to assume these principles as working hypotheses."
Plus the wager: each choice bets on consequences; refusing to think about it
is also a wager. Status: VERIFIED-via-gochema-audit (book free online;
confirm wording at video stage). Use for: the honest epistemics beat.

## Moment-to-moment, timelessly

**R7. Duration needs unchange + change; birth is present (Ñāṇavira FS
Dynamic §2; Ñāṇamoli "birth is present"; SN 12.67 two sheaves).** Lamp plus
clock-tick make duration; representation of spatial structure is not itself
spatial — same move for time. "From a phenomenological point of view our
birth is always here with us... birth is present." Two sheaves of reeds:
pull one, the other falls. Status: VERIFIED-via-gochema-v1.2-audit (FS text
not separately on thomas shelves; do not requote beyond these lines without
fetching FS text). Use for: across-scales thesis + stopwatch beat.
