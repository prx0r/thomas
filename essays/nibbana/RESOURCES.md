## Fire goes out, not somewhere else

**F1. Gone out, not gone somewhere (Aggivacchagotta, MN 72 / MN I 484).**
Fire burns dependent on fuel; with exhaustion of fuel "it comes to be
reckoned as gone out" (nibbuto tveva sankham gacchati) — asking which
direction it went is malformed, not unanswered. Chinese Madhyama parallel
holds the same structure. Status: VERIFIED (standard sutta + Analayo course
note; box scaffold arguments file). Use for: annihilation objection.

**F2. Lamp verse (Ratanasutta).** "Nibbanti dhira yathayam padipo — the wise
get extinguished even like this lamp." Status: VERIFIED (box scaffold,
machine-checked excavation). Use for: one-line fire image.

**F3. Arahants' own words (Theragatha/Therigatha).** "Sitibhuto'smi nibbuto
— I am grown cool, extinguished as I am." Status: VERIFIED (box scaffold
10-passes). Use for: coolness, not blankness.

**F8. Warming vs cooling (Magandiya, MN 75).** The worldling warms himself
beside hot embers trying to cool down; the arahant cools down. Warming and
cooling share a word, opposite processes. Status: VERIFIED (box scaffold
arguments file). Use for: why sex and drugs lose.

## Craving's end IS the goal, not its prize

**F4. Destruction-of-craving as synonym (Dhammacakkappavattana, SN 56.11).**
Third truth: "the complete fading away, cessation, giving up, relinquishment
of THAT very craving" (tassayeva tanhaya asesaviraganirodho). Nanananda
Sermon 1: tanhakkhayo is used AS nibbana in the suttas; the commentarial
"khayamatta na nibbanam" (destruction alone is not nibbana) and the
"nibbanam-agamma" (on-reaching-nibbana) periphrasis are later inventions.
Status: VERIFIED (Bodhi SN 56.11 public translation + box scaffold).
Use for: goal mechanics.

## Two modes, same extinction

**F5. With and without fuel-remnant (Itivuttaka, two Nibbana-elements).**
Sa-upadisesa: arahant still living, senses run, no grasping. Anupadisesa:
final breakup, no remainder. Same extinction, different residue.
Status: VERIFIED (standard text + box scaffold). Use for: soil remains
while alive; no waking after final.

## Bare sensing, no adder

**F6. Bahiya (Ud 1.10).** "In the seen only the seen, in the heard only the
heard..." No experiencer added; with no you in it, no you anywhere.
Status: VERIFIED (standard text + box scaffold). Use for: what changes —
taking stops, sensing stays.

**F7. Non-manifestative consciousness (Kevaddha DN 11; Brahmanimantanika
MN 49).** "Vinnanam anidassanam, anantam sabbato pabham" — where earth,
water, fire, air find no footing; with cessation of consciousness all this
ceases. Nanananda reads anidassana as non-manifestative, consciousness
unestablished on any object, not a metaphysical ground. Analayo cautions the
textual variant (paham/pabham) and parallels lacking the luminous phrasing —
do not build an Absolute on it. Status: VERIFIED with caution flag (box
scaffold + Analayo course notes). Use for: observer-falls beat; keep the
caution in a footnote or aside.

## Here, not elsewhere

**F11. World's end in this body (Rohitassa, AN 4.45 — verify number in draft
pass).** The end of the world is found in this fathom-long body with its
perceptions and mind; no travelling needed. **Kalakarama attitude (AN 4.24 —
verify number):** the Tathagata sees without grasping what is seen.
Status: STANDARD refs, flag check:true — confirm numbering against an index
before video stage. Use for: foreignness beat; no elsewhere.

## Stream entry bounds

**F9. Seven lives at most (AN 3.86 + parallel SA 820).** Stream-enterer
reborn seven lives at most; once-returner once here; non-returner once in
Pure Abodes; arahant: birth destroyed. Status: VERIFIED (Analayo rebirth
text, local box). Use for: correcting the watering stage.

## Wholesome still plants

**F10. Merit-arrived consciousness is still puthujjana's (Nanavira §15).**
Puññupaga, apuññupaga, anejupaga consciousness — intentions of merit,
demerit, imperturbability — determine consciousness as arrived-at-merit etc.,
and "as puthujjana's determinations they are a condition for puthujjana's
consciousness." Status: VERIFIED (nanavira.org webfetch). Use for: maxed-out
wholesome is still becoming; only taking's end ends planting.

## The gap practice can use tonight

**F12. Two arrows (Sallatha, SN 36.6).** Struck by feeling, the uninstructed
adds sorrow and resistance — two pains; the instructed feels the first,
not the second. Status: VERIFIED standard text. Use for: smoking beat's
space-then-choice; feeling without second arrow is the small-scale nibbana
already verified.
