## What was NOT done (and why)

- No full anidassana unpack (F7 kept to observer-falls minimum + caution).
  The ramble never goes there; a full Kevaddha treatment would be verbatim
  discourse-unpacking, which the loop rule forbids.
- No tathata/Rohitassa/Kalakarama unpacking. Same reason. They are shelved
  for the foreignness beat if ai2 needs them; F1+F5 already answer it.
- Source-code model corrected, not replaced. Replacing it with "the texts
  say process" would preach; showing the storage implication has no textual
  home while keeping generate-for-continuity lets the reader keep what works.
- No Levin in this draft. The ramble's "medium for pattern to take form"
  tempts a bioelectric gloss, but that would be my writing, not the masters'.
  Levin stays shelved until a ramble beat actually requires non-genetic
  patterning — flagged for the rebirth-simulation piece, not this one.

## Sharpening left for ai2 (the seed/germination upgrade)

1. The seed line is the draft's weakest textual link. Nanananda Sermon 4
   gives the exact upgrade, already verified on this box: "Kamma is the
   field, consciousness is the seed, craving is the moisture." One sentence
   maps the whole garden: soil = field, apple seeds = consciousness-seeds,
   watering = present craving wetting them. Replace the generic "seeds can
   sprout" with this and the metaphor becomes canonical, not decorative.
2. Germination conditions can then sharpen "stop watering" precisely: water
   is present craving at feeling-tone, warmth is attention, season is the
   six-base encounter. Each maps to a checkable moment (F12's gap), so the
   practice stops being metaphorical at exactly the point the smoker already
   understands it.
3. Sela's seed riddle (same sermon: sown seed feeds on taste of earth and
   moisture, grows; aggregates likewise by cause) could carry the "last guy
   who owned the house" beat — same seed, no owner. Optional; use only if
   the beat needs weight.
