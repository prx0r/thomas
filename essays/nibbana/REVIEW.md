   view, doubt, clinging to rites; lust, aversion, conceit continue, new seeds
   still plantable, seven lives at most (AN 3.86). Only arahantship ends all
   planting. Fix: stream entry = first verified seeing + watering weakens +
   season now limited; final stopping belongs to the arahant.
2. **Stored source-code profile.** No text posits a held file between lives;
   tendencies are dispositions in process, no storehouse (Yogacara alaya is
   the later exception, not early doctrine). Fix: keep generate-button image
   for continuity-without-identity, cut the storage implication.
3. **Ending happiness.** Stopping becoming ends happiness-that-must-be-fed,
   not peace. Fix: name the two happinesses apart — rented relief vs
   hungerless cool (Magandiya warming vs cooling, MN 75).
4. **Waking to a clean garden next life + helping others after.** After final
   breaking up there is no next waking and no helper left over. Fix: helping
   belongs to the living arahant; the final is no more fuel, not a better
   morning.
