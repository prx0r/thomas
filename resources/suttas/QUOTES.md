## S1. Wandering without first point — SN 15.1 (Anamatagga Samyutta, opening of SN 15.1-20)

Pali: "Anamataggo'yam, bhikkhave, samsaro. Pubba koti na pannayati
avijjanivarananam sattanam tanhasamyojananam sandhavatam samsaratam."
Bodhi: "This samsara is without discoverable beginning. A first point is
not discerned of beings roaming and wandering on hindered by ignorance and
fettered by craving." Verified: stock formula, cross-checked public
translations. Use for: no-first-cause answer.

## S2. Ignorance defined — MN 9 (Sammaditthi); SN 12.2 §15 (Vibhanga); SN 35.80 (Avijja Sutta)

"Not knowing about suffering, not knowing about the origin of suffering,
not knowing about the cessation of suffering, not knowing about the way
leading to the cessation of suffering — this is called ignorance."
Verified: Ñanamoli/Bodhi MN 9; Bodhi SN 12.2; Thanissaro SN 35.80; triple
cross-check. Use for: textual definition of avijja. Note MN 9's loop:
with taints arises ignorance, with ignorance arise taints.

## S3. Dependent arising, full sequence and cessation — SN 12.2

"With ignorance as condition, volitional formations... consciousness...
name-and-form... six bases... contact... feeling... craving...
grasping... becoming... birth... ageing-and-death, sorrow, lamentation,
pain, grief and despair. Such is the origin of this whole mass of suffering.
With the remainderless fading away and cessation of ignorance comes
cessation of volitional formations..." etc. Verified: Bodhi SN 12.2.
Use for: the chain the essay's metal detector unpacks; quote only the
links needed, never the whole.

## S4. Against the stream — MN 26 (Ariyapariyesana)

The Dhamma as deep, hard to see, "patisotagami" — going against the stream.
Verified: standard rendering. Use for: why the outside view grates.

## S8. Conception needs three; consciousness does not transmigrate — MN 38
(Mahatanhasankhaya, Thanissaro ATI)

"The descent of the embryo occurs with the union of three things... mother
& father... her season... a gandhabba is present." Same sutta corrects
Sati: no "same consciousness" runs and wanders. Verified: box file.
