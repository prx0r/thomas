## NN1. Going round vs going out — Sermon 3

"With the help of the simile of a whirlpool, we attempted an explanation of
the terms samsara on the one hand, and Nibbana on the other, that is to say
'going round', or samsarana, and 'going out', or nissarana." Use for: the
essay's canonical definition.

## NN2. Abyss = painful bodily feeling — Sermon 3 (via SN 36)

"Monks, abyss is a synonym for painful bodily feelings... When the
uninstructed worldling is contacted by a painful bodily feeling, he sorrows,
grieves, and laments... This is called an uninstructed worldling who has
not risen up in the bottomless abyss." Use for: why beings keep delighting
in sense pleasures knowing no other way out.

## NN3. Thirst from feeling — Sermon 3

"The thirst of craving arises in beings in various forms of existence
because of painful feeling." Use for: one-line sandcastle mechanism.

## NN4. Theme verse (whole series frame) — Sermon 1

"This is peaceful, this is excellent, namely the stilling of all
preparations, the relinquishment of all assets, the destruction of craving,
detachment, cessation, extinction." Use for: nibbana essays; the Direction
the whirlpool exits by.
