# Essay-sharpening skill — from vagueness to certainty

Adapted from the gochema pushing work (Logicvid sessions, PUSHING_GUIDE,
AUTONOMOUS_PUSHING_AGENT_SPEC) and the Corpus Hermeticum trial decomposition.
Deliberately lighter: no formal graphs, no truth-packets, no proof engine.
One job only — take an essay's claims and metaphors and sharpen them until a
hostile reader cannot blur them.

## When to use

Every ai round, after the draft exists, before PROCESS-ai<N>.md is written.
Run it on each load-bearing claim and each working metaphor. Not on setup,
not on transitions, not on the owner's voice lines.

## The procedure

### 1. Name the claim in one sentence, owner's words

Quote the essay's own sentence. If you cannot isolate one sentence, the claim
is not a claim yet — flag it and move on. No paraphrase at this step.

### 2. Find the load-bearing step

Ask: which word, if false, collapses the sentence? That is the step to push.
Everything else is decoration and waits its turn.

### 3. Expand metaphors to their logical ends

Run the image until it breaks. For each element, write the mapping in plain
words: this stands for that. Then push past comfort:
- What does each element correspond to in the mechanism? (soil = capacity,
  seed = tendency, water = present craving)
- Where does the mapping fail? (soil cannot be removed without killing the
  garden — but the texts never ask that; the failure locates the essay's
  overclaim, not the metaphor's charm)
- What does the failure reveal? The break point is the insight. Write it down
  — it is usually the next paragraph.
- A metaphor that survives full expansion without breaking is load-bearing.
  One that breaks early gets cut or demoted to illustration-only, flagged as
  such in PROCESS.

### 4. Steelman, then destroy

State the strongest version of the claim first — stronger than the essay put
it. Then one hostile paragraph: the relabelling accusation adapted — "this
is poetry, not a mechanism; restate it without loss or concede it." Repair
only what survives. Record the kills in PROCESS-ai<N>.md.

### 5. Licensed vs unlicensed

For every borrowed claim, write both halves in one breath: what the passage
licenses, and what it does not. A quote that proves the near point does not
prove the far one. Mark the gap; never bridge it silently.

### 6. Certainty ledger

Tag every load-bearing claim, in PROCESS not prose:
proven (text states it) / supported (premises resolve, no live objection) /
plausible (coherent, objection stands) / asserted (owner's intuition, no
source yet). The essay body never carries the tags — but nothing tagged
asserted may be written as if proved.

### 7. Sharpness test

Read the final sentence aloud and ask: could a hostile reader restate it
without loss? If yes, keep. If it needs your tone to survive, rewrite until
it stands naked. Sharpness is survival without style.

## What this is not

- Not the full pushing method. No inquiries folders, no root.json, no graph
  growth, no formal passes. Those serve a translation pipeline with a proof
  engine. Here the essay is the product and the PROCESS file is the audit trail.
- Not verbatim unpacking. The masters' words enter where the essay's claim
  needs them — confirm, correct, deepen — then plain rendering, per house rule.
- Not endless. One pass per ai round over load-bearing claims and metaphors
  only. The sharpening queue in PROCESS carries the rest forward.
