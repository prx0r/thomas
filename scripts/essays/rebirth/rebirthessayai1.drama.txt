It is referring to one process that takes place at various scales. Obviously you have physical rebirth — the heart stops beating and there is the continuation of tendencies to the next life. There are various interpretations of how this happens; the common idea is that some process links one life to the next. Start there, and then shrink it until it fits inside a single moment of anger. If the same structure holds at both ends, you are looking at one process, not two doctrines.

The artist
The physical body dependently arises along with karma and all the tendencies and potentialities — I don't really know what that means yet, so take the small version first. Birth on the smallest possible scale is the coming into form of an abstract entity. Much like an artist gives birth to art from ideas: the artist transforms the abstract into physical, using the physical to transmit the idea to another, who by interacting, by exposing their consciousness to it, receives the idea. Art is a medium for transmission. Ask how an idea becomes physical: the artist paints it, or writes it. The artist IS the medium by which the abstract becomes physical.

[soft pause] One correction before this carries weight, and it matters. There is no realm of ideas the art is downloaded from, no realm of code it is uploaded to. Patterns live in determinations, not in a storehouse — the same cut made to the source-code model in the nibbana essay. What the artist shows is narrower and stronger: abstraction becomes shareable only at contact. By reading poetry it causes you to feel emotion; it takes you somewhere not contained in the physical marks. That somewhere is caused by the meeting of a prepared human — English, culture, a heart — with the physical thing. The interaction between consciousness and the physical entity gives rise to the experience, perhaps the one the artist had. The artist transmits their state of mind through the art, letting another experience a different perspective. So birth, at every scale, is this: something abstract becomes experienceable where a prepared meeting happens. No meeting, no birth.

Restart: realms right now
Buddhist rebirth makes sense when you say it happens across scales. Being reborn in the hungry ghost realm is akin to saying: I now feel like a hungry ghost, and I am right now a hungry ghost. Radical, but checkable. Moment to moment you are continually reborn; no part of you remains the same apart from the story that says I am the same. So rebirth is when I make contact with something that has the potential to make me angry — I get angry — and now I have been reborn in a hell realm. Because in this exact moment I have become anger. I am the emotion: wrathful, explosive. The whole point of the no-self teaching is that I am not a person who is angry; there is rebirth into the realm of anger. Buddhist practice is the training of the mind to come into contact with triggering circumstances and not get reborn into these hell realms — or even these heavenly realms. Take the opposite: you encounter something that makes you happy. You find a million gold coins. There is birth into excitement. Perhaps it lasts a while and then the excitement dies. That is birth and death.

[soft pause] Rebirth as doctrine just means the process of becoming, persisting, and decaying. It applies as a timeless principle to this very moment and to a whole human life. You are born, you persist, you decay — just as an emotion is born, persists and decays. So the ending of rebirth means the end of being reborn in emotional states like anger or excitement. What about joy or love? Upon seeing a million pounds, in the enlightened one there is still the arising into excitement — but it is allowed to naturally resolve and die. The nature of birth and death are seen so clearly as dependently arisen that in the exact instance excitement arises, it naturally ceases. Whereas in the unenlightened one, excitement arises, it is taken to be me as an excited person, then endless fantasy about what I might buy, the updating of identity to rich person, endless further activities before the impending death.

[soft pause] You see: the birth has started the stopwatch. You can't escape death after birth. Your joy, your happiness, upon being born, are surely destined to die. Doesn't that make them all the more beautiful? In the same way your suffering and despair — there is only so long they can persist. Birth and death oscillate, giving the illusion of temporality and solidity. In truth it is one timeless process. In death, the seed of birth has already been planted — which here means something precise and unmystical: inside becoming, the push toward next footing is already operative, because craving is the sustenance even between stations. When a being sets this body aside and is not yet reborn in another body, the texts designate it as craving-sustained, for craving is its sustenance at that time. No passenger named, only fuel named.

Verify it tonight
If this is true it is a structure you can verify right now. Meditate more, then meet what historically made you angry, and watch the probability of being reborn into anger drop. Freedom comes from zooming in and slowing down, not from getting somewhere else. You see, you don't choose to be reborn into anger, therefore you are trapped by it. The truth is you don't choose what makes you angry — this is your karma. You arrive with tendencies, dispositions which in truth aren't you. Knowing why something makes you angry doesn't stop the anger. What stops it is only developing the space by which, for a brief second, you see the anger occurring all by itself. It never had anything to do with you. This is the end of rebirth: the end of cyclic existence, of being pulled into afflictive and joyous emotion, because they are seen as naturally occurring processes. Once seen, it becomes impossible to grasp them as me or mine. It would be like grabbing sand — it just slips through your fingers.

[soft pause] Note what this concedes and what it doesn't. It concedes the trigger is not chosen; the groove was laid by conduct, not by this morning's intentions. What actually conditions becoming is what was lived during the drive-phase, not a deathbed flash. It does not concede helplessness: at the feeling-tone, the second arrow can be unfired. That gap is the whole laboratory, tonight, no metaphysics required.

The whole-life scale
Now apply this process to a whole human life. The heart stops and the death process is underway — a process, not a switch; even the transplant glossaries say so. In Tibetan accounts, during the dying the body of masters decays slower than normal. The physical body shutting down removes the noise of the senses, and the master is said to influence their next birth. This doesn't fit the moment-to-moment analogy cleanly, and the essay will not pretend it does. Treat it as reported: a slowed process with less noise, claimed to allow steering. The mechanism argued here needs none of it — fuel seeking footing is enough — but the report stands as the tradition's own laboratory note, neither proof nor nothing.

[soft pause] The working model, stated plainly: there exists the possibility of birth for you the way there exists the birth of an idea. You are reborn after physical death because what you are is not born and does not die the way a self would. Much like you become anger — it exists and dies — you become this human being — it exists and then dies. All the while it was never truly you. It is just a natural process that occurs. Conception itself is described as needing three: parents, season, and the descending stream — no meeting of all three, no birth. Nothing travels; conditions meet.

[pause, section change]

The frog questions, left standing
In the next life I may be a particularly greedy frog. I am not sure how this all maps. If I am a good Buddhist in this life and reduce craving, why does that lead to a favourable chance of rebirth? Is it luck to be born human after being a frog, or was it a particularly saintly frog? The questions stand because the ledger model is false, and replacing it with nothing would be evasion. Karma is momentum plus conditions, not reward points: present volition is one condition among many, emphasised because it is the one you can move, and fruition can arrive long after the deed, once conditions ripen. No one can hand you the frog's biography. What can be said: less grasping now means less fuel later, whatever costume the next footing wears. The rest is the seam, marked, not smoothed.

[pause, section change]

Memory, distrusted properly
The primary evidence offered is meditators claiming past-life recall in deep samadhi. The whole chain is claimed to be seen — yet memory is not reliable enough to count as evidence. And there is a sharper worry the ramble names honestly: is it not a case of the mind generating an experience to match what it believes it should be seeing? That worry is legitimate and it has a textual answer with teeth: remembering is reconstruction under present attitude, false memories feel exactly as real as true ones, and regression work may heal without proving anything. So no: a vivid past-life image is not evidence by itself. Checkable detail or it stays a story. The essay keeps the gold standard from the samsara piece — felt gold is not verified gold.

[soft pause] Yet if physical rebirth is true, it reshapes what this life is. The interpretation that holds all of this together: rebirth is continually happening right now in your very perception of the moment, built from habits and interpretations. By weakening the mechanism of rebirth right now in this moment, you actively weaken it at every scale, for all beings. The simple process of looking earnestly at the nature of things as they arise generates momentum — not only to end rebirth for yourself, but as example that plants in everyone you contact. Not substance transferred. The gold bar handed, the mirror lent: what spreads is the worked example, and worked examples are how the outside view travels from hand to hand.
