# Shelf: Michael Levin lab (developmental bioelectricity)

Public papers (Tufts Allen Discovery Center). Summaries below are plain-word
paraphrase for essay use; cite paper + year, never present as proof of
rebirth — Levin shows non-genetic patterning, not post-mortem continuity.

## L1. The bioelectric code — Levin, Cell 2021 ("Bioelectric signaling:
Reprogrammable circuits underlying embryogenesis, regeneration, and cancer")

Non-neural cells form electrical networks via ion channels and gap junctions
that store and process target-morphology information. Voltage maps can
diverge from and dominate genetics: rewriting them induces whole organs
(e.g. eyes) in wrong places. Formal use: pattern information outside the
DNA sequence; form guided by fields, not just genes.

## L2. Memory without its brain — Shomrat & Levin 2013 (planaria), reviewed
in Pezzulo et al. 2021

Flatworms trained on a task, decapitated, regrow new brains — and retain
the learning. Bioelectric states persist over a week in tissue as rewritable
memory. Formal use: dependence without identity — function outlasts the
hardware that ran it. Does NOT show transfer between bodies.

## L3. Ingressing minds — Levin, PsyArXiv 2025 ("Ingressing Minds")

Genes plus environment underdetermine form; evolution exploits a structured
space of patterns ("Platonic space" in his mathematical sense) that ingress
via physical pointers like bioelectric networks. Research program, not
result. Formal use: the respectable version of "information from outside" —
bounded to development, no womb-homing mechanism claimed.
