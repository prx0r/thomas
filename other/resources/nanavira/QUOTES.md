# Shelf: Ñāṇavīra Thera (Clearing the Path / Notes on Dhamma, 1960-65)

Full text: Path Press volume; A Note on Paticcasamuppada read verbatim.
Cite note + section (§7, §10, §23-25).

## N1. Dhamma is checkable now — §7

"He who sees the Dhamma sees paticcasamuppada; and he has also said that
the Dhamma is sanditthika and akalika, that it is immediately visible and
without involving time." Use for: the essay's test — verification now, not
belief later.

## N2. Being = taking self at face value — §10

"The puthujjana takes what appears to be his 'self' at its face value; and
so long as this goes on he continues to be a 'self', at least in his own
eyes (and in those of others like him). This is bhava or 'being'...
The arahant has altogether got rid of asmimana... and does not even think
'I am'. This is bhavanirodha, cessation of being... Quite clearly, the idea
of re-birth is totally irrelevant here." Use for: maze definition. Flag the
closing caveat openly — Nanavira brackets rebirth from structural seeing.

## N3. Ignorance conditions itself — §§23-24

"Paticcasamuppada depends upon non-seeing of paticcasamuppada... Avijja
depends upon avijja... though it is impermanent and constructed, avijja is
so stable." Use for: the hard crux; no first point because self-maintaining.

## N4. Judge and accused — §25 (via MN 2 Sabbāsava views)

"However much a man may step back from himself to observe himself he cannot
help taking avijja with him. There is just as much avijja in the
self-observer as there is in the self-observed... In reflexion avijja is
the Judge as well as the Accused, and the verdict is always 'Not Guilty'...
That is why the Dhamma is patisotagami, 'going against the stream', it gives
the outside view inherently unobtainable by unaided reflexion." Use for:
centerpiece; graft-as-method. The three views it cites: with self I perceive
self; with self I perceive not-self; with not-self I perceive self.
